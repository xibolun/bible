## History

- FHL CBOL, Old and New Testament original-language Chinese dictionaries,
  published by 信望愛資訊中心 and its contributors. Original transparent copies:
  https://bible.fhl.net/public/dhfhl.tgz and
  https://bible.fhl.net/public/dgfhl.tgz . Retrieved 2026-11-17;
  source hashes are in `data/chinese-dictionary-manifest.json` in the Bible repository.
- Bible Chinese Dictionary Edition, 2026, normalized and published by
  xibolun / Bible contributors. Decode dump field/newline escapes; preserve
  Traditional Chinese wording, source references and entry numbers. Empty
  records and supplemental letter-suffix codes are omitted from the lookup
  index. Complete original source dumps remain unchanged in this directory.
  Source authors are not responsible for the transformations and do not
  endorse this application.
